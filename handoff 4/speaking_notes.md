# ClassPulse AI: Timed Speaking Notes (5-7 minute demo)

**DEMO MODE - synthetic data.** Draft for the team to rehearse and edit. Target length: about 6 min 45 s.

Bracketed [screen cues] are placeholders. Match them to what Persons 2 and 3 actually built once you rehearse.

| Time | Segment | What to say | Screen cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | Opening | "A teacher can look at a classroom of 30 students, but they cannot see 30 minds." | Title / first screen |
| 0:30-1:00 | The problem | Right answers hide different reasoning, and wrong answers hide different reasons. Today the teacher usually finds out after the lesson. | [Same screen or a short slide] |
| 1:00-2:00 | One live question | Ask the physics question: "At the highest point of a ball thrown upward, what is its acceleration?" Students answer and explain briefly. | [Teacher question and student answer views] |
| 2:00-3:00 | The class picture | The teacher sees the answers grouped. A shared misconception shows up: "zero velocity means zero acceleration." Say clearly that the app only names it when the student's own words support it, and shows uncertainty otherwise. | [Teacher dashboard] |
| 3:00-4:00 | Teacher approves | The AI drafts an intervention. The teacher reviews it, edits it, and publishes it. Nothing reaches students without teacher approval. Read the Socratic prompt: "If the acceleration were zero at the top, what would the ball do next?" | [Intervention draft, then publish] |
| 4:00-4:45 | Students retry | Students see the teacher's message and a hint, "At the highest point, does gravity stop acting?", then answer again. | [Student retry screen] |
| 4:45-5:30 | Paired result | The same 10 students, before and after: 60% → 90%, +30 percentage points, calculated by the app from paired data. Be honest: one student is still struggling, and one correct retry is not proof of lasting mastery. | [Before/after metric] |
| 5:30-6:15 | One student's evidence | Open one student's Mistake → Master map to show the personal evidence behind the class number. | [Mistake → Master map] |
| 6:15-6:45 | Close | ClassPulse helps the teacher understand and support each student. | Closing screen |

## Say out loud at least once

- "This demo uses synthetic data; there are no real students."
- "The percentages are calculated by the app from the paired answers."
- "The teacher stays in charge of what students see."

## Do not say

- That the students "mastered" the idea.
- That every learner was fixed.
- Any percentage that is not on screen from the app's calculation.

## Backup plan

- Keep these notes as the backup script. Anyone on the team should be able to present from them.
- If the live demo fails, use a screen recording (see the rehearsal file for the recording checklist).
