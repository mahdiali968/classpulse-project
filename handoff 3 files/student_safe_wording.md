# Student-Safe Wording and Retry Prompt

**DEMO MODE - synthetic data**
For Person 3 (student UI). Nothing here contains the private answer key.

## Retry prompt

> "Your teacher just shared an idea about this question. Try again: at the highest point of a ball thrown upward, what is its acceleration? Explain in one sentence."

## Hint (approved candidate from Handoff 1)

> "At the highest point, does gravity stop acting?"

## Feedback lines after the retry

| Retry result | Student-safe wording |
|---|---|
| correct | "Nice work: your reasoning matches the physics for this question." |
| partially_correct | "You're close. Add which direction it points and why." |
| incorrect | "Not there yet. Try the hint again and think about what gravity is doing at the top." |
| insufficient_evidence | "Give it a try in your own words, even if you're unsure." |

## Wording rules

- Do not say a student has "mastered" the idea. One correct retry is not proof of lasting mastery.
- Do not show other students' answers or names.
- Keep the DEMO MODE - synthetic data label visible in the demo.
